
===== orderflow
SUMMARY: I tested 7 order-flow method families on the 10 instruments, with volume backed out of the VWAP and a bar-shape delta proxy (there is no bid/ask data). Real order-flow signals added nothing: aggression, absorption, exhaustion/climax volume, CVD divergence, low-volume retest confirmation and volume-profile POC/value-area/LVN all fell inside a random-veto null or hurt. One filter met every protocol rule: the location of the signal within the VWAP standard-deviation bands. With 'Skip a long when the signal bar closes more than 2.4 sigma above the session VWAP (short: below)', and keeping the skipped setup as an untaken virtual trade so it still blocks the next signals, TEST results improve as follows. Expectancy per trade at the 2R exit rises from +0.180 to +0.262 (paired-by-day 95% CI of the difference [+0.008, +0.150]; the shared lib boot_diff CI is [-0.119, +0.308]). At the 1R exit it rises from +0.059 to +0.112, and at 1.5R from +0.099 to +0.153. TEST hit rates for 1R / 1.5R / 2R rise from 53.0/44.0/39.3% to 55.6/46.1/42.1%. On ALL data 75% of trades are kept, total R is +180 vs +178, and 8 of 10 instruments improve. This directly targets the complaint about buying at the top of a stretched swing. Caveat: an equal-weight sigma (no volume) works just as well, so the gain comes from how stretched price is from the VWAP, not from volume. Second best is the session CVD-proxy direction (do not trade against the session's net delta). It improves 9 of 10 instruments and TEST 2R expectancy to +0.233, but its CI includes zero, so it is promising only. Combining it with the sigma filter gives TEST +0.319 but keeps only 60% of trades and total R falls to +170.

===== location
SUMMARY: I tested price-location and regime filters at the signal bar against the user's complaint that the indicator buys tops and sells bottoms. All of them were added to my own copy of the model, /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Bank-Loan-Report/21ed2550-acec-5cf1-bf01-a24cbf22fe18/scratchpad/research/location/combo_loc.py (options loc, locMax, locBlock, skipLossDir, after2Loss, deepExt; all off by default; with no options it reproduces the baseline exactly: n=1190, E2 +0.150). The features are in .../research/location/feats.py and are causal: each uses only bars up to the signal bar, and the 15m/1h timeframes use only completed bars.

None of the location filters works. The data go against the complaint: signals at the extreme are not the losing ones. On TRAIN, removing the most extended or strongest signals hurt (dropping CMO>62.5 cost -0.135 R/trade, Stochastic>91 -0.081, being at the top 15% of the session range -0.075, RSI7>80 -0.068). The extension caps the user would expect to help were flat to negative on TEST: VWAP-sigma cap -0.005, VWAP-ATR cap -0.061, ADX cap -0.082, consecutive-close cap -0.018, Donchian-top cap -0.022. Requiring 15m or 1h trend agreement hurt (TEST -0.106 and -0.151). Momentum floors that looked good on TRAIN failed on TEST (mom10 TRAIN +0.044 but TEST -0.110; Stochastic floor TRAIN +0.037 but TEST -0.048).

The main reason is structural. With one trade at a time, a blocked signal is replaced by a later signal that would otherwise have been skipped, and those replacement trades averaged about -0.13 R. Blocking the rest of the leg after a rejected signal did not fix this. Placing the limit deeper when the signal is extended also hurt on TRAIN (E2 0.08 to 0.12 against 0.131).

Two methods had positive TEST results; neither is statistically proven:
(1) skipLossDir: after a losing trade, block same-direction signals for the rest of the session until a trade wins. TEST E2 +0.269 against +0.180 (+0.089, CI [-0.105, +0.293]). It also improved TEST at 1R (+0.040) and 1.5R (+0.069). It kept 80% of trades, improved 8/10 instruments, and total R stayed the same (+178). But TRAIN was flat (+0.006, and slightly worse at 1R and 1.5R). Almost all the gain is in October.
(2) pdRoom: skip a long whose close is more than 3.4 ATR above the previous session high (mirror for shorts). The gain is small but the same sign in both halves: TRAIN +0.018, TEST +0.017 (CI [-0.179, +0.225]). It kept 88% of trades, improved 7/10 instruments, and total R moved from +178 to +175.
Both technically meet the counting criteria, but every bootstrap CI includes zero. Each trade's result swings by about ±1.46R, so at these sample sizes a difference of 0.1 R/trade cannot be told apart from noise, and about 800 variants were screened.

===== entry
SUMMARY: Better entries and trade management (key=entry). I tried 145 variants, picking thresholds on TRAIN only. None of the entry changes passes the protocol: deeper Fibonacci, impulse-leg or VWAP limits, ATR-pullback limits, structure stops, confirmation entries and ATR stop multiples 1.2-2.4. All of them lower TRAIN expectancy, and most lower TEST expectancy too. The reason is adverse selection. 51% of baseline winners never trade more than 0.6 ATR against the entry, and 73% never more than 1.0 ATR, while every loser does. So a deeper limit drops the winners and still fills the losers.

The useful finding is management: move the stop to breakeven at +1R. On TRAIN, expectancy at the 2R exit rises from +0.131 to +0.157. On TEST it is flat (+0.177 vs +0.180; difference -0.003, CI [-0.185, +0.177]). Total R rises from +178 to +198 and PF from 1.24 to 1.35. Full -1R losses fall from 62% to 47% of trades (TEST: 61% to 47%), max drawdown from 30R to 25R, and the longest losing streak from 20 to 8 (scratch trades break streaks). The cost is that 2R wins fall from about 38% to 32%.

Combined with a 3-bar retest window (instead of 5), the result passes the protocol on paper: TEST at the 2R exit +0.195 vs +0.180 (CI [-0.163, +0.191]), 1R +0.075 vs +0.059, 1.5R +0.113 vs +0.099, 8/10 instruments, total +212R. But the retest-window values around 3 are noisy on TRAIN (2: 0.113, 3: 0.142, 4: 0.123, 5: 0.131), so I treat that part as luck. Partial take-profit at 1R, time stops, early close-stops and fixed 1R/1.5R targets all reduce expectancy. No method's TEST improvement is statistically significant: every bootstrap CI is about ±0.18R wide.
