 SUMMARY: I found a rule that switches the stop to the tight mode, and it beats the oracle-stop target: the full engine now scores 75 exact / 5 miss / 7 extra (baseline 71/9/9, oracle stops 74/6/6). It gets all 8 tight trades right. On those 8 trades the engine's stop prices are within 0.013 ATR of the measured ones, and the exit bar and outcome match exactly.

The rule is not trade-history based. A trade gets the tight stop when two things are true:
1. Its displacement is the first displacement candle of the current session in that direction. This counts every qualifying candle, even ones that were never armed because of position, cooldown or the first-bar/Sunday-UTC gates.
2. The previous session's candle closed against that direction by more than 1 ATR (close below open for a long, above open for a short).

The tight stop is entry -/+ max(0.9*ATR14, distance to the displacement candle's low/high). Otherwise the stop stays at 1.8*ATR14.

This settles the NIFTY/SENSEX 10-09 paradox. SENSEX printed a bullish displacement at 09:35 (armed, never filled), so its 10:05 long was not the first of the session and kept the normal stop. NIFTY had no displacement before 10:05, so its long was tight.

Stop classification on all 80 trades is 77/80. On the oracle-tight history (74 trades with a recorded arm) it is 71/74, and inside the rule's own engine run it is 71/75. Four normal trades are wrongly made tight: CRUDE 10-08 10:50 L, NASDAQ 10-12 06:20 S, NIFTY 10-12 09:55 S, and in-engine only US30 10-07 05:30 S. The rule makes about 11.6% of all engine trades tight; the original shows 10%.

- Threshold: any previous-session body threshold from 1.0 to 2.75 ATR gives the same 75/5/7 and 77/80. Without a threshold (any opposite candle) it drops to 74/6/8 because of NIFTY 10-06 09:50 L, whose previous candle body was only 0.08 ATR.
- VWAP-slope condition: it must be part of the displacement definition, otherwise EURUSD 10-09 15:30 and GC 10-09 18:25 lose their tight stop.

```
var float pO = na, var float pC = na, var float sO = na
var bool bullSeen = false, var bool bearSeen = false
if newSession

    pC := close[1]
    sO := open
    bullSeen := false
    bearSeen := false

tightS = bearEvt and not bearSeen and (pC - pO) > 1.0*atr

if bullEvt

if bearEvt
    bearSeen := true
```
